<p class="page-description">We used to grow and learn in spaces that were built on tight-knit connections and deep relationships. Today, we grow embedded in networks, mediated by technology, and shaped by algorithms.</p>

<p class="page-description">In environments where we have more information and less certainty, more connection and less cohesion - leaders are needed less for answers and more for presence. Attention has become the scarcest resource. Trust, the most valuable currency.</p>

<p class="page-description">My work focuses on trust: how it's built, how it's broken, and how it's changing in a world where individuals manage thousands of weak ties, groups are replaced by platforms, and organisations function more like ecosystems than hierarchies. </p>
