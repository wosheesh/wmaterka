<div class="section about-intro">
    <div class="about-text">
        <p class="page-description">Wojtek Materka is Adjunct Professor of Organisational Behaviour at INSEAD, where he teaches leadership, organisational behaviour and ethics on the MBA, GEMBA and MIM programmes and has twice received the award for outstanding teaching of an elective. He directs company-specific leadership programmes for global organisations across Europe, Asia, Australia and the Middle East, and teaches on INSEAD's open-enrolment executive education.</p>

<div class="section">
    <p class="page-description">His work is on trust, transitions and uncertainty, and draws on the group relations tradition. Before academia he spent two decades in business, leading the launch of the first online credit card in Central Europe and later advising organisations across Asia-Pacific and Europe on innovation and digital transformation. He holds a PhD on the development of trust in technology-mediated relationships and an MBA from INSEAD. He is Polish and Australian, and works between Australia, Singapore and Europe.</p>
